Domain: Boarding Rules & Passenger Conduct
(Use as: boarding_rules_dataset.docx)
Boarding rules are established to ensure passenger safety and smooth train operations. Passengers are generally advised to arrive at the railway station well before the scheduled departure time. Boarding should be done from the designated platform and coach as mentioned on the ticket or displayed on station boards.
Reserved passengers should enter only their allotted coaches, while unreserved passengers must travel in unreserved compartments. Boarding a moving train or standing near coach doors is unsafe and strictly discouraged.
Passengers are expected to follow queue discipline during boarding and avoid overcrowding. Luggage should be carried responsibly without obstructing others. Certain hazardous or prohibited items are not allowed during travel.
Special assistance is generally available for senior citizens, disabled passengers, and passengers with children. Ticket examiners may verify tickets during or after boarding.
Following boarding rules helps prevent accidents, ensures fair seat usage, and improves the overall passenger experience
