 Domain: Ticket Types & Travel Basics
(Use as: ticket_types_dataset.docx)
Railway ticket types are designed to support different travel needs of passengers. A General or Unreserved Ticket allows passengers to travel without a reserved seat and is commonly used for short-distance or daily travel. A Reserved Ticket provides a confirmed seat or berth in a specific coach and class.
A Tatkal Ticket is intended for last-minute travel and is available for booking closer to the journey date, subject to general railway rules. A Waiting List Ticket indicates that a seat is not yet confirmed, while a RAC (Reservation Against Cancellation) ticket allows limited travel with partial seating arrangements.
A Platform Ticket permits non-travelers to enter railway platforms for assisting passengers. A Season Ticket is issued for frequent commuters traveling regularly between two stations.
Railway tickets also specify travel classes such as Sleeper Class, AC Class, and General Class, each offering different comfort levels. Tickets may be issued as e-tickets or counter tickets, both serving the same travel purpose.
Passengers are required to carry valid identity proof while traveling on reserved tickets. Traveling without a valid ticket may result in penalties according to railway rules. Ticket checking is performed to ensure compliance and maintain orderly travel.
